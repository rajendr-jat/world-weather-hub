Understand the Weather Around You

Learn how weather works, how forecasts are created, and how to understand the data behind every forecast.

1. What Is Weather?

Weather means the condition of the atmosphere at a particular place and time. It can change from hour to hour and day to day.

WEATHER

Short-term atmospheric conditions.

CLIMATE

Long-term average weather patterns over many years.

2. Weather Data Explained

Temperature

The measure of how hot or cold the air is, typically shown in Celsius (°C) or Fahrenheit (°F).

Feels Like

How the temperature actually feels to your body when humidity and wind speed are factored in.

Humidity

The amount of water vapor in the air. High humidity can make heat feel much more uncomfortable.

Rain Probability

The percentage chance of measurable precipitation occurring during the forecasted time period.

Wind Speed

Measured in km/h or mph, it shows how fast the air is moving. Strong winds can impact safety.

Wind Direction

The direction the wind is blowing from (e.g., North, East, South, West).

Air Pressure

The weight of the atmosphere. Falling pressure usually indicates an approaching storm.

Cloud Cover

The percentage of the sky that is covered by clouds at any given time.

Visibility

How far you can see clearly. Fog, dust, and heavy rain can severely reduce visibility.

UV Index

A measure of ultraviolet radiation strength. Higher values mean faster skin damage.

3. How Weather Forecasts Work

Modern forecasts combine observations, mathematical models, and supercomputers.

1. Weather Stations

Collect ground data.

2. Satellites

Monitor clouds from space.

3. Weather Radar

Detect precipitation size & speed.

4. Prediction Models

Supercomputers run math formulas.

5. Data Processing

Meteorologists interpret the data.

6. Forecast Generation

Delivered to your device.

4. Understanding a Forecast (Example)

This is sample data, not a live forecast.
  • Temperature: 32°C (The actual air temperature)
  • Feels Like: 35°C (It feels hotter due to humidity)
  • Rain Probability: 70% (High chance of rain today)
  • Humidity: 78% (The air holds a lot of moisture)
  • Wind: 18 km/h (Moderate breeze)
  • UV Index: 7 (High risk of sunburn, use protection)
  • AQI: 92 (Air quality is acceptable/moderate)

6. Air Quality and AQI

AQI (Air Quality Index) tells you how clean or polluted the air is. It measures pollutants like PM2.5, PM10, Ozone, and Nitrogen Dioxide.

0-50: Good
51-100: Moderate
101-150: Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups
151-200: Unhealthy
201-300: Very Unhealthy
301+: Hazardous

7. Extreme Weather Facts

Heatwave

Extended periods of excessive heat.

Thunderstorm

Accompanied by lightning, strong winds.

Heavy Rain & Flood

Excessive water overflowing land.

Cyclone

Severe rotating storm system.

Cold Wave

Rapid and dangerous drop in temperature.

Drought

Prolonged shortages in the water supply.

8. Weather Safety Checklists

During Extreme Heat

  • Stay hydrated
  • Avoid prolonged direct sunlight
  • Check local warnings

During Thunderstorms

  • Stay indoors when possible
  • Avoid exposed locations
  • Follow local safety guidance

During Heavy Rain

  • Avoid flooded roads
  • Follow local warnings
  • Take care near fast-moving water

Poor Air Quality

  • Check AQI levels
  • Reduce prolonged outdoor exposure
  • Follow local health guidance
