Cookie Policy

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your actions and preferences over time.

How We Use Cookies

Preference Cookies: We use local storage to remember your searched city or location so you don't have to re-enter it every time.

Advertising & Third-Party Cookies: We use Google AdSense to serve ads. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies directly through your browser settings. You can also opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings.
